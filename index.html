Session setup

Focus: 10 min–24 h · Break: 1–60 min · Long break: up to 1.5 h · break is always kept ≤ focus.

Why it works

Short, bounded sprints lower the activation cost of starting and protect attention from fatigue. Pair each focus block with one method — e.g. recall cards, or a Feynman write-up.

How to import into Google Calendar
  1. Click Download .ics above to save the file.
  2. Open Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export.
  3. Choose the .ics file, pick which calendar to add it to, and click Import.
  4. Your study blocks appear as weekly recurring events. (Re-import after you tweak the schedule to refresh.)

The assignment helper works offline with guided templates. To get real AI-written summaries/tips, paste your own API key — it's stored only in this browser and sent straight to the provider.

Why do AI calls fail?

Nine times out of ten it's CORS, not your key. A browser will only let this page call another site if that site explicitly allows it. Two things commonly block it:

  1. You opened the file directly. If the address bar starts with file://, your page has no real origin and browsers refuse every API call. This is the most common cause. Fix it by serving the folder locally — in a terminal, run python -m http.server 8000 in the folder holding this file, then open http://localhost:8000/study_buddy.html.
  2. The provider doesn't allow browser calls. Some APIs are server-only and will refuse no matter what. Anthropic works (this app sends its browser-access header) and Gemini/Groq/OpenRouter generally do; others may not. If one refuses, try another provider — OpenRouter reaches many models behind a single key.

Running a Local provider? Ollama needs OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* set before it will accept requests from a web page.

Hit Test connection above for the exact error. AI is optional — everything else works offline.

Why can't it use Claude's key for free?

This page runs entirely in your browser with no server behind it, so there's no shared key it can borrow. Adding your own key (or running it through the templates) are the two safe options. Keys here never leave your machine except to call the provider you chose.

Connecting real Google Classroom later

Step-by-step (when you're ready)
  1. Create a free project at console.cloud.google.com.
  2. Enable the Google Classroom API under APIs & Services.
  3. Configure the OAuth consent screen (External, add yourself as a test user).
  4. Create an OAuth Client ID of type "Web application". Classroom blocks file://, so this only works on the deployed site.
  5. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, enter the site's origin only — scheme + domain, with no path and no trailing slash. For this app that is https://study-buddy-10baa.web.app. Google rejects any path here, and the browser only reports the origin anyway, so that is all it needs.
  6. Leave Authorized redirect URIs empty — the in-browser Google Identity token flow this app uses doesn't need one.
  7. Use scope classroom.coursework.me.readonly with Google Identity Services to fetch assignments.
  8. Paste the Client ID below and press Connect on the Classroom page — the demo data is swapped for your real courses.
💡 Getting “Invalid Origin: URIs must not contain a path or end with '/'”? That's this step — the field must be exactly https://study-buddy-10baa.web.app, with nothing after the domain.

Welcome to Study Buddy 👋

A quick tour of what each section does. You can reopen this anytime from the ❔ button on your dashboard.

▦

Dashboard

Your home base — today's study plan, day streak, cards due, and focus sessions at a glance.

⚑

My Goals

Write what you're working toward (e.g. “Get an A in Algebra 2”). Each goal gets a step-by-step path, and your goals power personalized picks in Learn and the Study Coach.

❏

Learn

Two tabs: Browse resources — hand-picked links per subject plus a source finder — and Study Coach, where you name a topic (like “the Civil War”) and get a guided plan: sources → practice → a test, with an AI coach checking in and answering questions the whole way.

⚯

Flashcards

Everything card-related in one place, with four tabs: Review (active recall + spaced repetition, now with a live progress counter and an end-of-session score), Subjects (browse each course's topics and cards), Create / Import (type your own cards or turn notes/files into cards with AI), and Weak Topics (the cards you missed, grouped by subject, with feedback on what to work on).

✓

Practice & Tests

Instant-feedback practice plus timed scored tests. Four difficulty tiers unlock in order: hit 90% on a test to open the Challenge; score 100% on both to open the Gauntlet ⚔️; ace every Gauntlet to unlock the ultra-hard Final Exam 🏆.

▰

My Progress

Every test is recorded here with your average, trend, weak topics to review, and strength by subject.

✎

Feynman

Learn by explaining a topic simply. Pick or type a topic, explain it plainly, find your gaps, then refine. A worked example shows you the goal.

◷

Pomodoro

A focus timer (default 30 min work / 10 min break). Study in sprints; the skip button unlocks only in the last 2 minutes of focus.

◫

Classroom

A demo of Google Classroom assignments with an AI helper that gives summaries, how-to steps, and tips.

◨

Assignments

A Kanban board for homework and projects. Add due dates, priority, status, and notes — and drag cards between columns as you progress.

◵

Schedule

Tell it your daily routine and commitments, and it builds a study plan around them. Click any block to edit it, and export the week to Google Calendar.

⚙

Settings

Set your name, add an optional AI key (powers AI flashcards, the assignment helper, and the source finder), and manage your data.

💡 Everything is saved right in this browser — no account needed. AI features are optional and only work if you add your own API key in Settings.
